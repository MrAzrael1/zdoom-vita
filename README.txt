This is a working prototype-ish build of ZDoom v2.8.1 ported to the PSVITA. Comes with some minor tweaks to the engine and a novel launcher GUI to adapt to the PSVITA.

This is something of a passion project by a former ZDoom/Skulltag modder and enthusiast of no particular importance. Releasing this publicly is my own small way of giving back to the homebrew community that has given me so much enjoyment over the years. Thanks from the bottom of my heart. <3  

To run this with anything outside of the bundled inclusions, you will require .wad data files from a legal copy of Doom, Doom 2, et cetera; they can be bought really cheaply on Steam these days. Comes with copies of Freedoom and the Doom 1 Shareware Episode since both are freely, legally redistributable.  

IWAD files (main game data .wad files for doom 1/2, tnt, plutonia, hexen, heretic, strife, etc.) go in ux0:data/zdoom-vita/iwad and PWAD files (mod files, custom maps, etc) go into ux0:data/zdoom-vita/pwad.

Overclocking to 500MHz is more or less required for any decent levels of performance.

Supports Hexen, Heretic, and Strife out of the box. Other IWADs like Chex Quest probably work fine too.

You can modify screen scaling and various other things under Options > Vita Settings. This includes the rear touchpad weapon-swapping behavior, and HUD resizing in the event of mods having giant custom HUDs. 2x scaling gives the most performance at the highest cost to visual fidelity.

Your mileage will strongly vary with mod support. As a general rule of thumb any ZDoom or Skulltag compatible mods released before 2016 might work based on my own testing. Keyword being might, especially with Skulltag. [Known issue: Any mods that require OpenGL graphics or more advanced UZ/GZDoom coding features that were implemented after ZDoom was discontinued just will not work currently. A fix for this may not be possible.]

Additionally as I know many looking here are brought by the promise of brutal doom: You may wish to wait a bit before getting too deep into a mod like that. The performance with that mod in particular is not great right now and while I am researching ways to squeeze more performance out of this port, it just may not ever be perfect.
